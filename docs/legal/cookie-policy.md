## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are used to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how you use a site. We also use related technologies (local storage, pixels, beacons, and SDK identifiers in our mobile apps), which we refer to collectively as "cookies" in this policy for simplicity.

## Why we use cookies

We use cookies to:

- Keep you signed in
- Remember your preferences (language, theme, region)
- Understand how the Services are used so we can improve them

- Detect and prevent fraud and abuse

## Categories of cookies we use

### Strictly necessary

These cookies are required for the Services to function. You cannot disable them through our preferences.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| better-auth.session_token (named __Secure-better-auth.session_token over HTTPS) | Keeps you signed in to the developer dashboard | 30 days, renewed while you use it |
| better-auth.state | Ties a GitHub or Google sign-in attempt to your browser to prevent forgery | About 10 minutes, during sign-in only |

### Functional

These cookies remember your preferences and improve your experience. They are not strictly necessary, but the site works better with them.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| pb-theme (local storage) | Remembers your light or dark theme choice | Until you clear it |
| pb-reader-settings (local storage) | Remembers your reading preferences, such as text size and verse numbers | Until you clear it |
| pb-reader-last (local storage) | Remembers the last chapter you read so the reader can resume | Until you clear it |

## Your choices

### Browser controls

Most browsers let you view, manage, and delete cookies. Look in your browser's settings for "Cookies", "Privacy", or "Site data". Note that disabling cookies may prevent some features from working.

### Mobile device controls

On iOS and Android, you can reset advertising identifiers and limit ad tracking through device settings.
